Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026.

Tether is a personal, single-user application. It is used only by its owner, to manage their own email, calendars and tasks. Only the owner can sign in, and only the owner's own Google accounts are connected. This policy describes what Tether does with the data it can reach.

Data Tether accesses

  • Google sign-in (openid, email): the account's email address and ID, to confirm that the person signing in is the owner and to label each connected account.
  • Gmail (gmail.modify): message metadata (sender, recipients, subject, date, labels) and short snippets, to build the inbox list; labels, to archive and unarchive threads when the owner asks; and message content, fetched when the owner opens a thread or asks their assistant to read or search mail. Tether does not send, draft or delete email.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, calendar.events): the list of the owner's calendars, and events on the calendars the owner selects, to merge them into one calendar; writing that merged calendar to one calendar in the owner's own account; and creating events the owner makes in Tether, including any guests, location, description and video link the owner adds.
  • Other calendars: a calendar feed (ICS link) the owner provides, read the same way.

How the data is used

Only to show the owner their own inbox, calendar and tasks, and to carry out actions the owner takes. Tether does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models. When the owner asks their own AI assistant to help with their inbox or calendar, Tether passes it the data that request needs, on the owner's behalf.

Tether's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is stored, and for how long

  • Message metadata, short snippets and links back to Gmail, for threads in the inbox window. Message bodies are not stored; they are fetched when opened and discarded.
  • Calendar events from the selected calendars, fetched for a window around today.
  • The owner's tasks and settings.
  • OAuth tokens, encrypted at rest.

Data is kept on a private server operated by the owner, reached only over HTTPS. Logs record technical events only, never email or event content.

Sharing

Tether does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to any third party. When the owner invites guests to an event, Google sends them the invitation, as it does for any calendar event.

Deletion and revoking access

Removing Tether's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions stops all access to that account at once. Turning a calendar off in Tether's settings deletes its stored events. The owner can delete any or all stored data from the server at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support email shown on Tether's Google sign-in screen.
